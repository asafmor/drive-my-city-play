Or Yam — Drive: credits and licences
=====================================

This file lists every third-party work in this game, its licence, where it came from and what was changed.
Full licence texts are at the end. The game's own code and the AI-generated textures and key art are not
offered under an open licence.

1. Map, imagery, elevation and weather data
===========================================
OpenStreetMap
  Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  world/world.json (roads, buildings, land use, trees and street furniture) is a database derived from
  OpenStreetMap. It is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  Changes: projected to local metres and combined with local survey corrections; one street renamed.

Sentinel-2 imagery (world/ground_mask.png, world/far_s2.jpg, world/hor_s2.jpg)
  Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026 (scene S2C_MSIL2A_20261110T082151, read through the Microsoft Planetary Computer).
  Copernicus Sentinel data is free and open (EU Regulation 1159/2013 and the Sentinel data policy).
  Changes: processed into the game's ground mask and far/horizon textures (tools/s2ground.mjs); ground_mask.png also uses
  OpenStreetMap land-use polygons (ODbL, above).

Elevation (world/terrain.bin, world/far.bin, world/hor.bin)
  AWS Terrain Tiles (Tilezen/Mapzen), https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
  * SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
  * Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
    (DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI, National Centers for Environmental Information).
  These are U.S. Government works, not subject to copyright protection within the United States.
  Changes: resampled to a local grid and fitted to elevations surveyed with GPS on the streets.

Live weather
  Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0, https://open-meteo.com/ (fetched live by your browser; unchanged).
